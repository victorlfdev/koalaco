## Platform

web

## Users

Small local business owners in Brazil — neighborhood shops, clinics, salons, gyms, restaurants — who want more customers and more sales through WhatsApp. They are not marketers or developers; they need a clear, no-nonsense partner who handles everything from strategy to execution.

## Product Purpose

Koala & Co is a marketing, sales, and technology agency for local businesses. The landing page communicates the agency's three-pillar approach (marketing strategy, sales conversion, web development), showcases past work, and drives visitors to book a free diagnostic session via WhatsApp. Success means qualified leads schedule a diagnostic call.

## Positioning

Koala & Co doesn't hand off work between separate departments. The three specialties — marketing, sales, and development — are run by three partners in-house from diagnosis to delivery. The only tool used for web is Next.js: fast, SEO-friendly pages without template limitations.

## Operating Context

- All work is done remotely, serving local businesses across any Brazilian city.
- Primary conversion channel is WhatsApp Business.
- Web deliverables are always built with Next.js (App Router).
- The landing page is the primary marketing asset for paid traffic campaigns.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Services**: Brand attraction & image (diagnosis, content, visual identity, photos/video), Conversion & technology (Next.js landing pages, local profile optimization, technical SEO), Scale & sales (paid traffic campaigns, conversion analysis, continuous alignment).
- **Process**: Four-step method — Diagnosis, Planning, Construction, Continuous optimization.
- **Tech stack**: Next.js (App Router), GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Lenis smooth scroll.
- **No LinkedIn presence** at this time.
- **No fabricated claims**: no testimonials, no case numbers, no "X clientes atendidos" — evidence must come from the user.

## Product Principles

1. **End-to-end ownership** — one team, one thread, no handoff between departments.
2. **Speed is strategy** — Next.js delivers fast pages that reduce ad spend waste.
3. **Results over vanity** — focus on actual sales and WhatsApp conversations, not likes or followers.
4. **Transparency** — client always knows which phase the project is in and what comes next.
